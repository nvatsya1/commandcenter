# Sample startup: MeridianOps

MeridianOps is a fictional Series B enterprise software company that uses AI agents to automate back-office workflows for mid-market insurance brokers.

## Materials provided

- Founded by two former engineers from a large enterprise software company.
- Product connects to policy administration, CRM, email, and document systems and automates repetitive servicing workflows.
- Claims 420 customers and $18M ARR, growing 85% YoY.
- Says gross retention is 94% and net retention is 121%, but cohort detail was not provided.
- Raised $32M in Series B at a reported $160M post-money valuation.
- Customers reportedly include regional insurance brokers and third-party administrators.
- Competitors include established workflow software, vertical SaaS products, and general-purpose AI automation platforms.
- Company says its advantage is a library of insurance-specific workflows and accumulated workflow data.
- Gross margin is described as "improving," with no exact figure.
- Management expects to reach $30M ARR within 18 months.

## Missing information

No customer concentration, CAC/payback, burn, cash balance, logo retention cohorts, implementation time, usage-based AI costs, pipeline conversion, or independent customer references were provided.
