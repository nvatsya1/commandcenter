# AI in VC — Portfolio Entry

## The setup

I built a personalized VC AI Workspace around a defined investment thesis: Series B enterprise AI, AI infrastructure, and applied AI software, primarily in North America with selective interest in India, Singapore, and China. The workspace uses persistent context, thesis criteria, evidence rules, and repeatable workflows so AI can support research and dealflow work consistently.

## Best deliverable

**VC Dealflow Screening Agent**

The agent takes startup materials and produces a structured first-pass screen: company and traction extraction, Fact / Inference / Unknown classification, thesis-fit assessment, technical differentiation analysis, diligence priorities, and an Advance / Watch / Pass recommendation.

The core insight is that AI is more valuable when it is given an explicit investment process rather than simply asked whether a company is “good.”

## Where AI is most useful in venture

**Highest leverage:**
- Synthesizing large amounts of company and market information
- First-pass deal screening and standardized note-taking
- Competitive and market research
- Extracting patterns across many companies
- Drafting investment memos, diligence questions, and research briefs
- Turning unstructured materials into structured datasets

## Where AI is least useful

AI is less reliable when the task depends primarily on judgment that is difficult to encode: assessing founder character, interpreting subtle interpersonal signals, deciding whether a market insight is truly non-obvious, building trusted relationships, and making final high-conviction investment decisions under uncertainty.

## My assessment

AI will not eliminate the investor. It changes where the investor spends time. The biggest opportunity is building systems that remove repetitive information-processing work while making the investor's judgment more informed, faster, and more consistent. The biggest risk is false confidence: an agent that produces polished analysis can still be wrong, incomplete, or based on weak evidence. My goal is therefore to build AI workflows with explicit sources, uncertainty labels, human approval points, and repeatable evaluation.

## What I learned

My strongest AI skill today is turning ambiguous research problems into structured workflows and prompts. My biggest gap is building more sophisticated autonomous systems with live tools, APIs, persistent state, and evaluation loops. After the program, I plan to keep building small agentic workflows around sourcing, screening, research, and financial analysis while becoming more comfortable with APIs, tool use, and production deployment.
